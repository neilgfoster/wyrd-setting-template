1. **Register** — the narrator's relationship to the world
2. **What institutions look like**
3. **How danger is stated**
4. **What a critical failure looks like here**, concretely
5. **Vocabulary** — words that belong, and words that do not
6. **What the joke is, and who it is on** — or whether there is one

Three pages of examples and one page of rules. Read at the start of every session.
